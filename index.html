ABOUT

Artist profile

REYES is a DJ, Producer, and label owner blending house, tech house, latin, and hip-hop influences.

With over a decade behind the decks, he brings together high energy grooves and underground club culture.

Beyond DJing, REYES creates events, develops artists, and produces music rooted in movement, culture, and authenticity.
